Takeaway

  • The identity \(1 + 2 + 3 + \cdots = -\tfrac{1}{12}\) is a regularized statement via the analytic continuation of \(\zeta(s)\).
  • In 2d CFT, this regularization gives the finite vacuum energy shift \(-c/24\) (or \(-c/12\) for \(L_0 + \bar L_0\)), which controls the ground-state energy on the cylinder and the modular properties of the partition function.
  • In bosonic string theory, the same zeta-regularized sum over oscillator modes leads to the intercept \(a = (D-2)/24\) and the critical dimension \(D=26\).
